Frequently Asked Questions

What does a property management company do in Johannesburg?

A property management company handles the operational side of a rental property. For short-term rentals, this can include listing management, pricing, booking coordination, guest communication, check-in and check-out support, housekeeping coordination and ongoing property operations.

Does Luxenest Properties manage Airbnb properties in Johannesburg?

Yes. Luxenest Properties provides short-term rental and Airbnb management services for property owners in Johannesburg and selected Gauteng markets.

Can Luxenest manage my property if I don't live in Johannesburg?

Yes. Our management model is designed to provide owners with a more hands-off experience by handling the operational requirements of the property on their behalf.

What types of properties can Luxenest manage?

We can work with suitable apartments, houses, villas and other residential properties that are appropriate for short-term rental management.

Do you manage more than Airbnb?

Yes. Luxenest supports multi-platform short-term rental management, including platforms such as Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo where appropriate for the property.

Can you help prepare my property for short-term rental?

Yes. Luxenest can assist with property presentation, styling, setup and listing preparation to help create a professional guest-ready space.

How do I find out if my property is suitable for short-term rental?

Contact Luxenest Properties for a property assessment. We can review factors such as location, property type, presentation and rental potential before recommending an appropriate strategy.
